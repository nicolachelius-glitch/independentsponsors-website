Approach

How we think about ownership.

Our philosophy is simple: back good businesses, partner with good people, and give both the time to compound.

Investment philosophy

We invest in businesses, not exits.

We back profitable businesses with strong management teams and clear opportunities to grow — whether by expanding into new markets, consolidating a fragmented industry, or building on a proven model. Each opportunity is chosen on its own merits, and we invest alongside our partners rather than from a blind pool.

Once invested, we take an active role. Drawing on decades of experience across operational management, opportunity sourcing and governance, we work closely with management teams on strategy, financial discipline, and growth — supporting the business rather than simply holding it.
