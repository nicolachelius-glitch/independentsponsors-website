Who we are

An independent sponsor, led by two operators who invest their own conviction first.

Independent Sponsors was founded by Bruce Chelius and Rob Katzen — both businessmen with decades of combined experience in private equity, venture capital, and managing companies. We invest deal-by-deal alongside our partners, choosing each opportunity on its own merits and staying actively involved in the businesses we back.

The model

What is an independent sponsor?

An independent sponsor finds and leads a deal first, then raises the capital for that specific opportunity — one investment at a time, rather than deploying from a pre-committed blind-pool fund. It's a model built on conviction: we only bring investors a deal we believe in enough to invest our own capital.

For business owners

An engaged, patient partner

You get investors who are genuinely involved — bringing operational, financial, and governance experience to the business — without the rigid timelines of a traditional fund. We hold for the time the business actually needs, and we're invested in getting it right, not just getting out.

For investors

Choice, deal by deal

You see each opportunity individually and decide on its own merits, rather than committing blind capital to a pool. That means full transparency on where your money goes, and the freedom to back the deals that suit you.

For everyone

Aligned from the start

We invest our own capital alongside our partners and are rewarded on performance. Our incentives are tied to the success of the business and the returns to our investors — everyone pulling in the same direction.
